 The non-standard options of <r>Find/Replace</r> dialogue are

   <b>Find by List</b> checkbox at <b>Find</b> field: allows finding words
     of "Find" field (words are separated by spaces)

   <b>Paste "Find"</b> button at <b>Replace</b> field: copies "Find" field
     to "Replace" field

   <b>RE2</b> button: allows applying additional regexp conditions
     to the search result

   <b>Redraw</b> button: switches to/from standard geometry of
     <r>Find/Replace</r> dialogue

   <b>-geometry</b> checkbox: if set, allows saving current geometry
     of <r>Find/Replace</r> dialogue at closing it

   <b>Replace by blank</b> checkbox: allows blank replacements,
     just removing the found strings

 Also, if some file tabs were selected after pressing Ctrl
 key and clicking them, <b>All in session</b> functions become
 <b>All in N files</b> where N is a number of selected tabs.

 The following hot keys are active in <r>Find/Replace</r> dialogue:
   <b>Ctrl+F</b> repeats the last search (originally <b>All in session</b>)
   <b>F3</b>     finds next occurrence
   <b>Ctrl+R</b> copies "Find" field to "Replace" field
   <b>Ctrl+T</b> returns to the current text
